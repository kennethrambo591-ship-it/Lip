Calculation Formula

VLDL = Triglycerides ÷ 5

LDL = Total Cholesterol − HDL − VLDL

Total Cholesterol = LDL + HDL + VLDL

Quick Guide

  1. Enter HDL and triglycerides.
  2. If VLDL is blank, the app estimates VLDL = TG ÷ 5.
  3. Enter either Total Cholesterol or LDL.
  4. The missing value is calculated automatically.
  5. If both TC and LDL are supplied, the app checks their mathematical consistency.
Educational / laboratory note: This calculator is a calculation aid, not a diagnostic tool. The TG÷5 VLDL estimate and Friedewald-style LDL calculation have limitations. Follow the laboratory report and clinical guidance. Direct LDL or another validated method may be needed in some situations, especially when triglycerides are very high.
